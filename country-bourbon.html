Country Bourbon draws you in with inviting vanilla and caramel, followed by the comforting richness of baked apple and honeyed oak. It’s an expressive, deeply satisfying pour that balances sweetness with the warmth and structure of mature bourbon.

There’s an easy confidence to it: flavorful enough to linger over, welcoming enough to share, and equally rewarding neat, over ice, or in a beautifully made Old Fashioned.

The whiskey comes first. Thoughtful selection and expert blending bring the elements together, while our proprietary Musically Mastered™ process becomes part of the final arrangement.

Some music feels familiar the first time you hear it. The best country songs have always had that gift.

Country music has a way of finding the extraordinary in ordinary moments—a great story, good company, and the kind of honesty that never goes out of style. That spirit is the inspiration behind Country Bourbon.

We begin with mature whiskey chosen for its generosity and depth. We build a blend where every element belongs, then invite music into the process through Musically Mastered™. The result is unmistakably its own, with a character that stays with you.
