PRIVACY
NOTICE.

This notice describes the information collected through the SHISHA SAMPLE website and how it is used.

Information you provide

When you send a Trade & Partnerships inquiry, we collect the contact, company, market and message details you submit. We use that information to evaluate and respond to the inquiry, including subsequent business correspondence.

The Drop List newsletter

When you sign up for The Drop List, we collect your email address, your confirmation that you are at least 21 years old, and your express consent to receive marketing emails about SHISHA SAMPLE releases, collaborations, products and availability. We process this signup through Netlify Forms and use the information to maintain our subscriber list and send the messages you requested. You can withdraw your consent using the unsubscribe instructions in any future marketing email or by asking for removal through our Trade & Partnerships contact form. We do not sell newsletter subscriber email addresses.

Form delivery

Our inquiry and newsletter forms are processed through Netlify Forms, which stores submitted information for site administrators and can send notifications to designated recipients. Please do not submit confidential or sensitive personal information through the form.

Optional website analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 only after you choose “Allow analytics.” You can decline or change that choice using “Analytics choices” in the footer. Google Analytics helps us understand visits, page engagement and site usage. We do not send your form's name, email, company or message to Google Analytics.

Age confirmation

The site asks visitors to confirm they are at least 21 years old. This is a self-affirmation; we do not request a date of birth. Your age-confirmation choice is remembered in your browser for a limited period.

Other services and links

The website may link to external sites and show third-party services such as embedded maps. Those providers have their own privacy practices.

Your questions

To ask about information submitted through the website, please use our Trade & Partnerships inquiry form.
