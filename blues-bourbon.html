BLUESBOURBONWHISKEY.

SOULFUL. EXPRESSIVE.
FULL OF CHARACTER.

Built from a high-rye mash bill and aged a minimum of 7 years in new American oak, Blues Bourbon leads with black cherry and warm cinnamon, then opens into toasted oak and dark toffee.

It is rich, expressive, and beautifully balanced—bringing together sweetness, spice, depth, and a long, rewarding finish in a way that bourbon drinkers immediately recognize as the real thing.

That flavor comes first. Then we carry it further through thoughtful selection, expert blending, and our proprietary Musically Mastered™ process.
